This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, used, and shared when you use Robo Routes. 1. Information We Collect Robo Routes does not require you to create an account and we do not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, or other contact information. The app may store gameplay progress, settings, purchases status, and similar game data locally on your device. 2. We do not receive your payment card information. - Apple Game Center: if you choose to use Game Center features, Apple may process your Game Center profile, scores, achievements, and related gameplay data according to Apple’s policies. 3. Advertising and Tracking The app may show ads through Google AdMob. Depending on your region and device settings, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads or tracking are enabled. You can limit ad tracking or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings. 4. Children’s Privacy Robo Routes is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action. 5. Data Sharing We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed by third-party services listed above for ads, analytics, fraud prevention, purchases, or platform features. 6. Data Retention Local game progress remains on your device unless you delete the app or clear its data. Data processed by third-party services is retained according to their own privacy policies. 7. Your Choices You can: - disable personalized ads where available; - manage app tracking permission in iOS Settings; - manage Game Center through your Apple account settings; - delete the app to remove locally stored game data. 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. 9.
